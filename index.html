The pub

10 rooms · sleeps 23 Est. 1866 Raglan's oldest Restaurant, Veranda, Garden & Sports bars downstairs 14 Bow St — you're in town

Harbour View Hotel, 14 Bow Street — colonial rooms above an award-winning bar, all refurbished, warm timber and good beds. Doubles, twins, a triple, a bunk room and a family room, so we can carve up 12 however we like. Shared bathrooms down the hall, no ensuites, no kitchen. Free WiFi, TV lounge, parking at the rear. Booking.com for Fri 9 – Sun 11 Oct, the room blocks for 12: ~$1,377 for the two nights — confirm exact total at checkout, and worth ringing them on 07 825 8010 first, they guarantee best rate direct and a 12-person group usually does better on the phone.

Fair warning for the pitch: you're above the restaurant in the middle of town, so expect some evening noise. Their own words — "it's all part of the Raglan experience".

The run sheet

FridayPat's → Raglan

The road trip down

We'll muster at Pat's Garage in Glenfield before the bus rolls out. First stop on the way down is the Rangiriri Hotel, a 160-year-old pub right by the motorway. From there it's on to The Backyard Gastropub in Whatawhata for a proper feed, a large garden bar with good pub food, before the final stretch into Raglan.

Straight off the bus and up the stairs at the Harbour View after that — bags in the room, then back down to the Garden Bar. Dinner's downstairs if we want it, or fish and chips from Jo's for anyone still hungry. Nobody has to drive, or walk, anywhere.

SaturdayThe big one

Golf, then the footy

Tee off mid-morning at Raglan Golf Club — a links course under Mt Karioi, forty-five dollars a round. From there it's a short drive to Hamilton for All Blacks v Australia at Te Rapa Tavern, home to New Zealand's largest pub screen plus around 30 more screens around the bar.

Te Rapa Tavern 30+ screens Bus there and back — no one driving
SundayRaglan → Pat's

Harbour, hot pools, then the road home

Take it easy — a harbour walk, a surf or a kayak for anyone keen, coffee in town. On the way out we'll swing by Waingaro Hot Springs — hot pools and a waterslide, about 20 minutes off SH23. Around $16 per person, with a group discount if we call ahead.

It's an older, no-frills spot with mixed reviews online, so worth a quick call ahead to check opening hours and that the waterslide is running.

Two more on the way back: the Rangiriri Hotel again, then the Pokeno Sports Bar for a last one before Auckland. Bus drops everyone back at Pat's Garage to finish where we started.
